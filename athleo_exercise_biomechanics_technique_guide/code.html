Kinetic Execution Protocol

3 PHASES
01

The Stance & Pre-Tension

RIGIDITY

Position feet hip-width directly beneath the bar. Soft-unlock knees to 15-20°. Pack lats down into your back pockets, gripping the bar just outside thighs. Take an intra-abdominal diaphragmatic breath and lock the ribcage.

02

The Hinge Descent

ECCENTRIC

Initiate by sending pelvis straight back as if tapping a wall 2 feet behind you. Knees remain fixed at original slight bend; shin angle stays strictly vertical. Bar grazes thighs down to mid-shin level until maximum hamstring stretch is achieved.

03

Concentric Hip Snap

DRIVE

Drive the floor away by contracting glutes forward through the floor plates. Finish tall with locked hips; strictly avoid hyperextending the lumbar spine backward at terminal extension. Exhale forcefully through teeth.

Fault Prevention & Cues

ERROR:

Squatting the bar downward with creeping knee flexion.

check_circle
TACTICAL FIX:

Lock knees in place after initial soft unlock. Think horizontal displacement (hips back), not vertical depth (hips down). Shins must remain plumb lines.

cancel
ERROR:

Spinal deflection / Lumbar kyphosis under load.

check_circle
TACTICAL FIX:

"Bend the bar around your shins" to pack lats. Keep sternum proud and chin slightly tucked. Stop range of motion the instant your pelvis halts backward glide.
